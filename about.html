The three layers of fragrance

A fine perfume reveals itself in movement. Understanding the three layers is the beginning of choosing well.

  • Top notes — the first impression

    Bright, volatile accords such as Calabrian bergamot, Sicilian lemon and spice that greet the skin in the opening minutes.

  • Heart notes — the personality

    Lush florals and aromatics — Grasse rose, jasmine sambac, iris — that define the character of the composition.

  • Base notes — the lingering memory

    Deep, persistent materials: bourbon vanilla, amber, sandalwood and oud, giving depth and the trail that stays.
